Home/Publication

Springer Proceedings

All the accepted and presented papers of ICVCCN 2026 are proposed for publication in a Springer book series, indexed in SCOPUS and Web of Science.

Publication is subject to peer review and final approval by the publisher.

Publication Process

  1. Peer review. Only papers presenting original content with novel research results or successful innovative applications will be considered for publication in the conference proceedings.
  2. Acceptance. Authors of accepted papers receive a notification along with reviewer comments.
  3. Camera-ready submission. Revised papers must follow the Springer template exactly and be accompanied by the signed copyright transfer form.
  4. Registration and presentation. At least one author must register and present the paper at the conference.
  5. Proceedings. Accepted papers are compiled and submitted to the publisher for production.
  6. Journal special issues. Selected high-quality papers may be invited for extended publication in journal special issues.

Reviewed By the Technical Committee

Thirteen technical committee members from Anna University, NIT Trichy, VIT Vellore, SRM University, Annamalai University, Kuwait University, IISc Bangalore, WIPRO and other institutions review every submission, advised by a ten-member advisory committee from Tata Advanced Systems, Meta, Intel, ZF, Apple and Infosys.

Author Requirements

Springer Template

Kindly ensure that your paper is formatted as per Springer guidelines, not exceeding 12 pages written in A4 size. Please refer to the Springer template on the Downloads page.

Copyright Form

A signed consent-to-publish form must be submitted with the camera-ready paper for every accepted contribution.

Similarity Index

Papers must clear the plagiarism check. Submissions exceeding the permitted similarity index will be withdrawn from the proceedings.
