About the Conference

The International Conference on VLSI, Communication, Computer Networks and Artificial Intelligence (ICVCCNA) aims to offer an interdisciplinary forum for discussion among researchers, engineers, and scientists.

It seeks to foster research and knowledge sharing in the fields of information and communications, as well as their related applications in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computing.
