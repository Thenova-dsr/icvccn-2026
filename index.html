About the Conference

The International Conference on VLSI, Communication, Computer Networks and Artificial Intelligence (ICVCCN) aims to offer an interdisciplinary forum for discussion among researchers, engineers and scientists.

The conference fosters research and knowledge sharing in the fields of computing, information and communications, as well as related applications in artificial intelligence, machine learning and computing. In addition to giving academicians, engineers and scientists a place to network, ICVCCN 2026 will inspire attendees to pursue advanced research in these priority areas.

ICVCCN 2026 is organised by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, AMC Engineering College (Autonomous), Bengaluru — an NAAC A+ accredited institution permanently affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi.

Publication

Springer Proceedings

All accepted and presented papers of ICVCCN 2026 are proposed for publication in a Springer book series, indexed in SCOPUS and Web of Science.

Publication is subject to peer review and final approval by the publisher.

01

Peer Reviewed

Every submission is double-blind reviewed by the Technical Programme Committee on originality, significance and clarity.

02

Hybrid Mode

Present on campus in Bengaluru or online — both modes carry equal standing and the same certification.

03

Journal Special Issues

Selected high-quality papers may be invited for extended publication in journal special issues.
