About the Conference

Home/About the Conference

An Interdisciplinary International Forum

The International Conference on VLSI, Communication, Computer Networks and Artificial Intelligence (ICVCCN) aims to offer an interdisciplinary forum for discussion among researchers, engineers and scientists.

In order to foster research and knowledge sharing in the fields of computing, information and communications — as well as related applications to artificial intelligence, machine learning and computing — the conference brings together contributions that cut across traditional disciplinary boundaries.

In addition to giving academicians, engineers and scientists a place to network, this conference will inspire attendees to do advanced research in the aforementioned priority areas.
