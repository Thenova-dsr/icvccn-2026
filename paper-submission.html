Paper Submission

Home/Paper Submission

Submission portal: all papers must be submitted online through the conference submission system. The link will be published here when submissions open. For queries, write to icvccn@amcgroup.edu.in.

Submission Process

  1. Prepare your manuscript using the Springer template — A4 size, not exceeding 12 pages, with a minimum length of 8 pages.
  2. Check originality. The work must be original, unpublished and not under review elsewhere.
  3. Submit online through the conference submission system with your chosen track.
  4. Peer review. Submissions are reviewed by the ICVCCN 2026 Technical Committee — thirteen experts from Anna University, NIT Trichy, VIT Vellore, SRM University, Kuwait University, IISc Bangalore and other institutions — on originality, significance and clarity.
  5. Acceptance notification is sent by e-mail to the corresponding author.
  6. Register and submit the camera-ready paper along with the signed copyright form.
  7. Present your paper at the conference in physical or digital mode.

Policies & Author Declarations

ICVCCN 2026 follows publication ethics as laid out and followed by reputed international bodies and societies such as COPE and Springer.

  • Any form of self-plagiarism or plagiarism from others' work(s) should not be present in an article.
  • If any model, concept, figure, table, data or conclusive comment from a previously published work is used in your article, you must properly cite a reference to the original work.
  • The language explaining it should not be the same as the language of the work from which it was adopted.
  • If you are using any copyrighted material, you must acquire prior permission from the copyright holder.

By submitting papers to ICVCCN 2026, authors make the following declarations:

  • The work submitted is the original work carried out by the author(s) of the paper.
  • All authors have made significant contributions to the reported work as well as to writing the paper (applicable where there is more than one author).
  • The submitting / corresponding author declares that they have obtained the consent of all co-authors before including their names in the paper.
  • The paper is not currently under review for publication in any other conference or journal, and it will not be sent to any other conference or journal for consideration while it is under review at ICVCCN 2026.
  • If the paper is accepted after review, one of the authors will register, attend the conference and present the work reported in the paper.
  • The author(s) assure the organisers that they will follow all the publication ethics of a peer-reviewed conference.
  • Assignment of papers for review is at the sole discretion of the Conference Organiser (CO).
  • Decisions on acceptance and rejection of papers are taken solely by the CO based on reviewers' comments.
  • The CO has full authority in preparing the technical programme and scheduling papers for oral presentation.
  • The CO reserves the right to publish details of accepted papers, as well as those of authors, on the conference website and other related forums maintained by the CO.
  • The CO has full authority regarding preponing or postponing the dates of any event associated with the conference, including paper submission, results announcement, conference dates and proceedings publication.
  • Photographs and videos taken during the conference will be freely used by the CO in multiple forums. By participating, authors and delegates give their consent to such use.
  • The CO is not liable to refund the registration fee of no-show papers.
  • If an accepted paper is removed from the proceedings due to quality issues, the CO is not liable to refund the fee.
