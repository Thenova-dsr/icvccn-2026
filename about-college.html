Home/About the College

AMC Engineering College (Autonomous)

AMC Institutions, established under the Paramahamsa Foundation Trust, are dedicated to providing quality education from Primary to Ph.D. levels.

Located on a 52-acre green campus along Bannerghatta Main Road, Bengaluru, the group includes AMC Engineering College (Autonomous), founded in 1999.

The college is permanently affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and holds prestigious accreditations including NAAC A+, NBA accreditation for three UG programmes in OBE format, and UGC 2(f) recognition.

Academic Profile

Programmes Offered

AMCEC offers 10 undergraduate and 5 postgraduate engineering programmes, along with MBA and MCA, and hosts seven VTU-approved research centres for M.Sc. (Engineering by Research) and Ph.D. programmes.

Curriculum & Disciplines

With a curriculum spanning Computer Science, Information Science, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Civil and Electrical Engineering, the institution blends academic rigour with practical exposure.

Research & Innovation

The college emphasises research, innovation and skill development, supported by state-of-the-art laboratories and infrastructure.

Holistic Development

AMCEC nurtures holistic development through technical clubs, cultural activities and sports. Strategic partnerships with industry and academia enhance student exposure to internships, projects and placement opportunities, fostering a dynamic and career-ready learning environment.
