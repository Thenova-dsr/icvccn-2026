AMC Engineering College (Autonomous)

AMC Institutions, established under the Paramahamsa Foundation Trust, are dedicated in providing quality education from Primary to Ph.D. levels.

Located on a 52-acre green campus along Bannerghatta Main Road, Bengaluru, the group includes AMC Engineering College (Autonomous), founded in 1999.

The college is permanently affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and holds prestigious accreditations including NAAC A+, NBA accreditation for three UG programs in OBE format, and UGC 2(f) recognition. AMCEC offers 10 undergraduate, 5 postgraduate engineering programs, MBA, MCA, and hosts seven VTU-approved research centers for M.Sc. (Engineering by Research) and Ph.D. Programs.

Curriculum

With a curriculum spanning Computer Science, Information Science, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Civil, and Electrical Engineering, the institution blends academic rigor with practical exposure.

It emphasizes research, innovation, and skill development, supported by state-of-the-art labs and infrastructure.

AMCEC also nurtures holistic development through technical clubs, cultural activities, and sports.

Industry Partnerships

Strategic partnerships with industry and academia enhance student exposure to internships, projects, and placement opportunities, fostering a dynamic and career-ready learning environment.
