About the Department

Home/About the Department

Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering

Established in 1999, the Department of ECE is one of the premier departments of the institution, dedicated to academic excellence, innovation and research.

The department offers an undergraduate programme, B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering, and two postgraduate programmes: M.Tech. in Digital Electronics and Communication Engineering and M.Tech. in VLSI and Embedded Systems.

It is supported by a team of highly qualified, experienced and motivated faculty members committed to delivering quality education and fostering a research-oriented learning environment.

Facilities & Strengths

⚙

Modern Laboratories

State-of-the-art laboratories and modern computing facilities give students practical exposure to emerging technologies and industry-relevant skills.

★

VTU Research Centre

The department hosts a VTU-recognised Research Centre, facilitating advanced research activities under qualified research supervisors and scholars.

✓

NBA Accredited

The B.E. (ECE) programme is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation under the Outcome-Based Education framework, reflecting a commitment to continuous quality improvement.

☆

Raspberry Pi Lab

An innovative lab giving students hands-on exposure to embedded platforms and prototyping.

☆

INNOTRONICS Lab

An industry-facing innovation lab providing real-world electronics exposure to students.

✎

Programmes

B.E. (ECE) · M.Tech. (DECE) · M.Tech. (VLSI & Embedded Systems) · Ph.D. research.
