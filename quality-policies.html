Quality Policies

ICVCCN 2026 follows publication ethics as laid out and followed by reputed international bodies and societies such as COPE and Springer.

  • All submissions undergo peer review by the Technical Programme Committee.
  • Papers are evaluated on originality, significance, technical soundness and clarity of presentation.
  • Assignment of papers for review is at the sole discretion of the Conference Organiser.
  • Decisions on acceptance and rejection are taken solely by the organiser based on reviewers' comments.
  • Only papers presenting original content with novel research results or successful innovative applications will be considered for publication in the proceedings.
  • Any form of self-plagiarism or plagiarism from others' work(s) should not be present in an article.
  • Reuse of any model, concept, figure, table, data or conclusive comment from a previously published work requires a proper citation to the original work.
  • The language explaining adopted material must not reproduce the language of the source work.
  • Use of copyrighted material requires prior permission from the copyright holder.
  • Reviewers with a conflict of interest are excluded from evaluating the concerned submission.
  • All submissions are treated as confidential documents throughout the review process.
  • Reviewer identities are not disclosed to authors.
  • Committee decisions are final and binding in order to preserve the integrity of the conference.
  • At least one author of every accepted paper must register and present the work at the conference.
  • Papers that are accepted but not presented are removed from the proceedings.
  • The organiser is not liable to refund the registration fee of no-show papers.
  • If an accepted paper is removed from the proceedings due to quality issues, the organiser is not liable to refund the fee.
