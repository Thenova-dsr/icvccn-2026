Call for Papers

Original and unpublished research papers, articles and working papers on topics related to the conference theme are invited for presentation and publication in the conference proceedings.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the four thematic tracks listed below. Papers that cut across tracks are welcome — indicate your preferred track at the time of submission.

Track 1

VLSI & Embedded Systems

  • VLSI Circuits and Systems
  • RF Circuit Design, Analysis and Testing
  • System on Chip (SoC)
  • Design of MEMS / Optical MEMS Devices
  • Thin-Film and Devices
  • Embedded Systems
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Nano Technology
Track 2

Communication & Networks

  • Wireless Communication
  • Wireless Sensor Networks
  • Computer Networks
  • Network Security
Track 3

Signal, Image & Video Processing

  • Audio & Speech Processing
  • Biomedical Signal Processing
  • Image & Video Processing
Track 4

Artificial Intelligence & Computing

  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Robotics
  • Distributed Systems
  • Cyber Security
  • Edge AI
  • Quantum Computing

Submission Requirements

Originality

Submissions must be original and unpublished. The paper must not be under review for publication in any other conference or journal while it is under review at ICVCCN 2026.

Formatting

Papers must be formatted as per Springer guidelines, not exceeding 12 pages in A4 size. Please use the Springer template available on the Downloads page.

Length

The standard paper length is 8 pages. Papers of minimum 8 pages on topics related to the theme are invited; over-length pages are charged separately (see Registration).

Review

All submissions will be thoroughly peer-reviewed by the Technical Programme Committee based on originality, significance and clarity.
