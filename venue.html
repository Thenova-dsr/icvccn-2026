Conference Venue

Home/Conference Venue

AMC Engineering College, Bengaluru

ICVCCN 2026 will be held on the 52-acre green campus of AMC Engineering College (Autonomous), located along Bannerghatta Main Road in south Bengaluru.

The campus offers modern conference facilities, state-of-the-art laboratories and comfortable presentation halls, and is well connected to the city centre, Bannerghatta National Park and the Electronic City technology corridor.

Address:
AMC Engineering College (Autonomous)

Getting Here

✈

By Air

Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) is approximately 60 km from the campus. Pre-paid taxis and app-based cabs are available at the airport.

●

By Rail

KSR Bengaluru City Junction (SBC) is about 20 km away. Yesvantpur and Banaswadi are the other major railway terminals in the city.

◆

By Road / Metro

The campus is on Bannerghatta Main Road with frequent BMTC bus services. The nearest Namma Metro station is on the Green Line at Silk Institute.

Location Map

Accommodation: the conference is non-residential. Delegates are kindly requested to make their own arrangements for accommodation. Several hotels are available along Bannerghatta Road and in Jayanagar and JP Nagar. For assistance, write to icvccn@amcgroup.edu.in.

Hybrid Participation

Unable to travel? ICVCCN 2026 is conducted in hybrid mode — registered authors may present their papers online. Joining details are shared with registered delegates ahead of the conference.
